Metrics Applicability in Recipe Invention (RAG)

            

              

                
Precision @ Recipe Invention

                

                  Ensures suggested recipes actually contain the requested ingredients (e.g., searching for "chicken" returns chicken dishes, not desserts). Low precision means useless recommendations with ingredients you don't have in your fridge.
                

              

              

                
Recall @ Ingredient Coverage

                

                  Measures the ability to find all possible recipes that can use the available ingredients. A high recall prevents missing ideal recipes because they are written differently in the database.
                

              

              

                
MRR @ Recommendation Priority

                

                  Verifies whether the most suitable recipe (which best matches the entered ingredients and restrictions) is positioned first in the results list, reducing search time for the user.
                

              

              

                
Latency @ Real-Time Assistant

                

                  The time needed to query the database and compose the context. A latency under 100 ms is essential to sustain a chatbot conversation while it invents recipes on the fly.
                

              

            

            

              Hybrid Fusion Utility: Lexical search (BM25) fails at synonyms or queries in different languages (e.g., "garlic" vs "usturoi"). By adding dense search (SentenceTransformer), the engine recognizes synonyms and associates culinary-compatible ingredients, providing much smarter recommendations.
